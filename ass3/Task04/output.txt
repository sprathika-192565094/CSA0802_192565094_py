=== Why sets cannot contain lists but can contain tuples ===

{('192311001', 'AI')} -> {('192311001', 'AI')} (a tuple of hashable items is hashable)

frozenset is hashable and can be an element: {frozenset({'b', 'a'})}
adding an existing element does nothing: {'a'} (an element is stored once because equal elements have equal hashes)
